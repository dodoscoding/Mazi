# Atlas Home and memory

Mode: Operate. One invited friend group rediscovers a memory, opens its photos and reads source context. This local experiment uses visibly synthetic fixtures.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Shared history becomes a curved, dated atlas; the selected memory stays large and readable beside it.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight blue ground, warm ivory serif lettering, fine blue cartographic lines, copper selection. Flat photographs and restrained rectangular controls.

STORY: Choose a dated photographic marker or equivalent list entry, open the selected photograph, explore its images and conversation, return to the same place.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop splits an upright curved date chart left and a large photograph right; title, date and Open memory sit below the photo. A three-item dated memory strip follows. Phone leads with the selected photo and action, then a compact chart and dated controls. Detail keeps the same photograph and puts context beside it or below on phones.

FORM: The Atlas of Us, bolder challenger notation-diagram-systems-star-atlas, seed 422333e9. Team selected it explicitly on 2026-10-03. Signature: selection retargets the chart's shallow orientation; opening carries the photograph into detail. No orbit or drag-only task.
