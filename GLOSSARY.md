# Mazi

Language for the shared history of one circle of friends.

## Language

**Annual recap**:
A collection of the circle's dated photographs from one year, arranged
chronologically by month.
_Avoid_: Generated story, highlight reel

**Conversation context**:
Imported messages that provide historical context for a photograph or moment.
A conversation can also belong to the shared history without any photograph.
_Avoid_: AI answer, generated recollection

**Past creations**:
Existing collaborative stories and time capsules retained after their creation
features retire. A sealed capsule remains unavailable until its opening time.
_Avoid_: Active stories, new capsules
